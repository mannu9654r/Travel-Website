Safari world & Marine park

Safari Park is the largest section of the park. It is a 50 minutes drive-thru through an eight-kilometer park replicating a Savannah habitat. The safari park houses large herds of Asian and African ungulates and a very large free flying waterbirds sanctuary.

Marine Park is the main park where various animal displays from all over the world are showcased. The main attractions of Safari World are located in Marine Park. Seven shows take place daily at different times, Dolphin show, Spy War show, Orangutan boxing show, Bird Show, Cowboy stunt show, Elephant show and Sea lion show. Other attractions at the Marine Park includea d Jungle cruise and Safari terrace

Tiger Kingdom

Tiger Kingdom Phuket is a once in a lifetime experience where you can meet, play, and have your photo taken with tigers! Watch the tigers as you kick back with some food and drink from our top-quality restaurant.You are sure to have a very memorable experience.

Phi phi Island

Phi Phi Leh and the Bida Islands are famous worldwide for their stunning colorful corals and marine life, these are the Phi Phi Islands' main dive sites. To the north of Phi Phi Don, two islands “Koh Mai Pai (Bamboo Island) and Koh Yung (Mosquito Island)” are also found, which makes the Phi Phi Island Group.

James bond Island

James Bond Island came into worldwide prominence in 1974 when it became one of the locations for the movie “The Man with the Golden Gun.” Thousands of tourists still visit this island, turning it into a pilgrimage for 007 fans and movie enthusiasts in general.

Krabi(Thailand)

Krabi 4 Island Adventure is the best way to explore the 4 Islands surrounding Krabi. The national parks of Chicken, Tub, Mor and Poda Island are absolutely stunning to see. We sail around these islands while enjoying drinks.
